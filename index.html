Social Media Use and Wellbeing

A latest Gallup study reveals some interesting patterns between adults social media use and wellbeing. The analysis reveals that more isn't necessarily worse, but less isn't necessarily better. A moderate use of social media appears to yield more optimal wellbeing outcomes.

The study classifies adults as low use (no use or less than 30 minutes a day), moderate use (30 minutes to less than two hours) or high use (two hours or more).
